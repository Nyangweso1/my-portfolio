04. Featured Projects

Built to Solve Real Problems

AI / Machine Learning

healthcare

A healthcare risk management system that predicts financial risk in healthcare insurance claims using logistic regression and advanced feature engineering.

The Problem

Healthcare insurance providers face escalating costs due to unpredictable claim patterns and shifting regulatory landscapes (SHIF/SHA in Kenya). Traditional actuarial methods fail to capture the complexity of individual risk profiles, resulting in poor premium pricing and exposure to catastrophic losses.

The Solution

I engineered a complete machine learning pipeline that ingests patient demographics, medical history, and claims data to predict individual risk scores. The system used Logistic Regression to achieve 93% classification accuracy, enabling data-driven underwriting decisions. The model was built with regulatory awareness—continuously adapting to evolving national insurance schemes to remain compliant and actuarially sound.

Technical Stack

Python Scikit-Learn SQL Pandas Machine Learning Data Pipelines
  • Data Engineering: Built secure, scalable ETL pipelines to ingest and validate financial and medical datasets.
  • Feature Engineering: Designed domain-specific risk features (hospitalization frequency, claim severity, comorbidity flags) with business logic alignment.
  • Model Training: Logistic Regression with cross-validation, achieving 93% ROC-AUC accuracy and robust generalization.
  • Regulatory Adaptability: Designed system to accommodate evolving insurance regulations (SHIF/SHA), demonstrating foresight for real-world deployment.
